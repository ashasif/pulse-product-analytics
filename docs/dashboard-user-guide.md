# Pulse Dashboard User Guide

## Purpose

Pulse is a synthetic product analytics and subscription intelligence
portfolio application.

The dashboard demonstrates an end-to-end workflow spanning data engineering,
semantic modelling, business analytics, randomized experimentation and
predictive decision support.

## Application views

### Executive Overview

Provides headline product, funnel, monetisation and retention KPIs.

### Growth & Acquisition

Shows acquisition-channel performance, marketing efficiency and platform
funnel evidence.

### Engagement & Monetisation

Shows feature-use activity, product engagement and successful billed payment
collection.

### Retention & Lifecycle

Shows maturity-controlled trial-to-paid conversion and paid retention through
D30, D90, D180 and D365.

### Experiments

Presents frozen Phase 5 randomized-experiment inference. The application does
not rerun the inferential analysis.

### Predictive Decision Support

Presents the locked Phase 6 Day-5 trial non-conversion prioritisation model.
The model provides modest ranking value and is not causal evidence.

### Methodology & Contracts

Exposes the canonical reporting metric registry and the principal analytical
interpretation boundaries.

## Source boundaries

Live descriptive business KPIs consume the validated PostgreSQL `reporting.*`
semantic layer.

Phase 5 inferential evidence and Phase 6 locked final-test evidence are loaded
from approved frozen repository artifacts.

## Synthetic-data disclosure

All Pulse customer behaviour is synthetic and created for portfolio and
learning purposes.

The application demonstrates methodology and engineering capability. It does
not imply that identical behavioural relationships would exist in a real
production population.
